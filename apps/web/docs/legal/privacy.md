## What data is collected and why?

When you subscribe to release notifications, we collect:

- **Email address** — to send you notifications when new versions of ngx-contract-kit are published.
- **Subscription date and IP address** — for security and fraud prevention.
- **Language preference** — to send communications in your preferred language.
- **Consent record** — the version of the privacy policy you accepted and the date.

We do not collect any other personal data. We do not use tracking pixels, analytics cookies, or third-party advertising.

## Legal basis for processing

Your data is processed on the basis of your **explicit consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you tick the acceptance checkbox and submit the form.

## Who has access to your data?

Your email address is stored securely in our database and processed through **Brevo** (formerly Sendinblue), our email delivery provider, for the sole purpose of sending you release notifications.

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with any other third party.

## How long is your data retained?

Your data is retained for as long as your subscription is active. When you unsubscribe, your email and associated data are deleted from our active subscriber list within 30 days.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your data at any time.
- **Withdraw consent** — unsubscribe at any time via the link included in every email, with no consequences.
- **Lodge a complaint** — with your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact [fran11799@outlook.com](mailto:fran11799@outlook.com).

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued subscription after a change constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
